Using Lookfold

Lookfold is a wardrobe and outfit journal intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is in preparation; this website does not accept registrations or purchases. Sign in with Apple is required to save personal records to an account and use online services.

Your content and AI previews

Only upload content that you have the right and necessary permission to use. You keep your rights to your content and allow processing needed for the features you request. Do not infringe others’ rights, use intimate images without consent, or bypass access or purchase controls. You decide what you actually wore. Plans and AI images do not count as wear records. AI results are visual suggestions, not assurances about body measurements, size, fit or comfort.

Free and planned Plus features

Manual wardrobe management, outfit creation, the calendar, wear history and reuse are free. Planned Lookfold Plus pricing is US $8.99 per month, with 35 fittings and automatic organization of 30 photos per billing cycle, and 5 GiB of account storage. When offered, Apple’s purchase sheet will show the actual price, currency and renewal terms. A subscription renews until canceled in Apple’s subscription settings. Viewing an existing result does not use a new fitting.
