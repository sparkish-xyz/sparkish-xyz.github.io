Questions about AquaTick

Is AquaTick free to use?
AquaTick is free to download and use with ads. Pro removes ads and adds optional iCloud sync for hydration records. Monthly and yearly subscriptions are available; check the app for current local prices.
Where can I log water?
Log water in the iPhone app, with Quick Add widgets, or on Apple Watch. Live Activity and Dynamic Island display progress only; they do not record water.
How many cups can I keep ready?
Cup Vault supports up to 6 favorites for Home Quick Add. Apple Watch has a separate layout setting with up to 6 cups, arranged in one or two columns.
How do Apple Health and iCloud differ?
Apple Health sync is optional and available without Pro. Pro’s optional iCloud sync keeps hydration records in sync across your devices. Cup photos stay on the device.
Do I need an account?
No account is required. Hydration records are stored on your device unless you enable optional syncing. The free version includes ads, and the app uses subscription, analytics and crash-reporting services; see the privacy details above.
Which devices and languages are supported?
AquaTick supports iPhone with iOS 26.0 or later and Apple Watch with watchOS 10.0 or later. The app is available in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Simplified Chinese and Spanish. This website is available in English, Korean and Japanese.
