FAQ

Does Google Maps work in Korea?

Google Maps is useful for planning, but walking and driving directions in Korea are limited. Locals rely on Naver Map and Kakao Map—this app helps you bridge saved Google places to those apps.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up. Recent places and favorites stay on your device.

Is the app free?

Yes, with ads on search and lists. The taxi card screen stays ad-free.
