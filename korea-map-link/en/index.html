How it works

  1. Input On Home, paste a Google Maps link, English name, or address—or share directly from Google Maps via the iOS Share Sheet.
  2. Verify Resolve parses your source, checks Naver/Kakao candidates, and ranks the best Korean match with confidence badges.
  3. Open Send to Naver or Kakao from the place ticket, save to Saved, or show the ad-free taxi placard from the Taxi tab.

FAQ

Does Google Maps work in Korea?

Google Maps is useful for planning, but walking and driving directions in Korea are limited. Locals rely on Naver Map and Kakao Map—this app helps you bridge saved Google places to those apps.

Can I share from Google Maps?

Yes. Choose Korea Map Link in the iOS Share Sheet to resolve a shared place into a Korean handoff ticket instantly.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up. Recent links and saved places stay on your device across Home, Saved, and Taxi tabs.

Is the app free?

Yes, with ads on search and lists. The taxi card screen stays ad-free.
