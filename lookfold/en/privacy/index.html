Scope

This notice describes data handling in the pre-release Lookfold app. The sample wardrobe is fictional. Saving a record to an account and requesting AI processing are separate actions; signing in does not send a photo to AI.

Sign-in and account storage

Lookfold uses an Apple sign-in identifier, app account ID and email if supplied by Apple for authentication and account separation. Credentials use the device Keychain. Drafts and cached content are kept locally. Garment and person photos, outfits, wear dates, plans, collections, sizes and notes you choose to save to an account are processed in a private Supabase database and file storage in the Tokyo region.

Selected photos and optional AI

The system photo picker imports photos you choose. Before each AI task you review its purpose and photo scope. Our server sends selected person and garment photos to OpenAI for a fitting, or the selected photo for garment classification. Outfit matching uses an outfit photo and up to 24 selected candidates, with their photos, IDs, names and categories. Private notes and payment details are not inputs to these requests. Requested results are saved to the account, not automatically added to actual wear history. Declining AI leaves manual recording available.

Purchases, support and sharing

When subscriptions are offered, Apple processes payment and RevenueCat handles purchase verification and purchase analytics. Lookfold keeps transaction, entitlement and fitting balance records linked to the account; it does not receive full card numbers. Support messages, related job IDs, errors and status help resolve requests. Support review of a photo requires your permission for that photo. Files you choose to share are handled by the recipient.

Access, retention and deletion

Use Account to export records, manage photos or request account deletion. Reauthentication with the same Apple account allows removal of stored app records and files and revocation of Apple sign-in authorization. Previously issued temporary photo links may work for up to 15 minutes; externally shared copies cannot be recalled. Provider logs, backups and required transaction records can have different retention periods from app content. Deleting the app or account does not cancel an Apple subscription. For access, correction, deletion or consent requests, contact alarmcrew.support@gmail.com.

This website

Sparkish product pages use Firebase Analytics to measure page visits. This website does not accept Apple sign-ins, wardrobe uploads, AI fitting requests or payments.
