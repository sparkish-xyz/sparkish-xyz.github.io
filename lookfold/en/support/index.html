Contact Lookfold

Lookfold is in preparation for Korea, the US and Japan. It has not been submitted for app review. For product questions or help with personal records, email alarmcrew.support@gmail.com. For an issue, start with the app version and the steps that led to it. Please do not send Apple credentials or payment details.

Fittings and wear history

Creating a fitting or planning a look does not increase the wear count. Confirm the date you actually wore it. When reporting a fitting in the app, permission to review the original person photo and the result are separate choices. Both start off.

Accounts and subscriptions

Saving to an account requires Sign in with Apple. In Account, use Photos & data to manage photos or export records, or Delete my account to start deletion after signing in again with the same Apple account. Manage an existing subscription separately in Apple’s subscription settings.
