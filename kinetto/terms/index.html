Terms of Use

Version 2026-09-12

KINETTO is a private run recorder with optional anonymous game participation. By accepting these core terms, you agree to use the service lawfully, provide only your own activity, and not interfere with the service, other participants, or privacy safeguards.

Your account and activity

Your KINETTO account is anonymous and tied to the device session. Keep access to your device secure. Run data can be checked for plausibility, duplication, safety, and reward integrity. KINETTO can withhold activity from game features when those checks fail or when privacy or safety controls require it.

Optional social features

Anonymous participation, Crew, Cheer, and Relay are optional. They do not expose your exact route or personal identity. Do not use those features to identify, track, harass, or manipulate another Runner. You can turn anonymous participation off in the app.

Service changes and controls

Core terms can be updated when the operating rules change; the app will ask for explicit acceptance of a new version. You can export your available account data or request deletion in the app. Some pseudonymized or restricted records may remain when required for safety, integrity, disputes, fraud prevention, or law.

Availability

Run recording and uploads depend on your device, permissions, network, and platform services. Keep a local copy of anything you need outside the app. These terms do not promise medical, coaching, emergency, or fitness advice.
