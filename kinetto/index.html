Before your first run.

Is KINETTO a running app or a game?

A running app first. You record and review your own runs; eligible activity then feeds into anonymous Runner connections, Crew growth, Cheer, and Relay.

Can someone find me through my Runner?

Ordinary Runner profiles do not reveal your name, face, exact route, real pace, or precise activity time. Crew activity is delayed and generalized.

Can my Runner join someone else’s Crew?

Yes. With Meet participation enabled, your anonymous Runner can be connected into other Crews. You do not see who connected you or how many connections you have.

What makes a Crew member grow?

Eligible, validated runs update that Runner’s shared Energy, Stamina, and Focus. Each connection separately develops its level, XP, Bond, and gear.

Can I message another Runner?

There are no free-form direct messages. Cheer and Boost let you offer in-app encouragement without disclosing your identity.

Does paying make my Runner faster or stronger?

Payments do not directly determine Relay power or reveal another Runner’s identity. Real pace is not directly converted into game power either.

When can I download KINETTO?

KINETTO is in development, with iOS first and Android to follow. Public release dates and download links will appear here when available.

Which languages and markets are planned?

English, Japanese, and Korean, with the United States, Japan, and South Korea as the initial target markets. Availability will be confirmed at launch.
