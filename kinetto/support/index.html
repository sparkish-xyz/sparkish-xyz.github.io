Support

Use the app’s Settings to review privacy choices, export data, request deletion, change anonymous participation, or report a safety concern.

Before contacting support

For a run that did not upload, keep the app installed and retry from its saved run history when you have a connection. Do not send exact route data, session tokens, or screenshots containing private health information unless support specifically asks through its verified channel.

Safety and privacy

Use the in-app report and block controls for unwanted interaction or suspected privacy exposure. An account deletion request stops access while the deletion process completes; the app shows its status when available.
