Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about AquaTick

What does AquaTick do?

AquaTick tracks water intake with one-tap logging from widgets, Live Activity, Dynamic Island, and Apple Watch—often without opening the app.

Can I log water on Apple Watch?

Yes. Use quick add buttons like +200mL and +300mL from your wrist, with complications for glanceable progress.

Does it support widgets and Live Activities?

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Live Activities, and Dynamic Island keep hydration visible and let you log in one tap.

Do I need an account?

No. AquaTick works without an account. Apple Health and reminders are optional, and AquaTick only uses the data you allow.
