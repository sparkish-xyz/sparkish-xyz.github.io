A few morning questions.

Good to know before your first shared alarm.

Can I download AlarmCrew now?

Yes. Download the iOS app from the App Store. Android is preparing for Google Play launch; its download link will appear here after release.

Can I use it on my own?

Yes. Set a personal alarm for yourself. When you want company, share an invite code to connect an alarm to your Crew.

Does following a friend share my alarms?

No. Mutual following and Crew membership are separate. Enter a shared 8-character invite code to join the Crew you choose.

Which devices will it support?

We’re preparing AlarmCrew for iPhone and iPad with iOS 26.0 or later, and devices with Android 8.0 or later. Allow alarm and notification permissions. On Android, also check exact alarm and full-screen notification access, volume, and battery settings.

Do alarms need an internet connection?

Registered alarms run on your device. Signing in and syncing Crew status need an internet connection. Alarms cannot ring when the device is powered off. Signing out removes scheduled alarms on that device. Each alarm follows the device’s local time.

How can I manage my account and data?

Sign in with Apple and set a nickname. Change your search visibility in your profile, block or report people, or delete your account in Settings. The app includes ads. Read the Privacy Policy for details on account, wake-up, advertising, and diagnostic data.
